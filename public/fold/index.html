This scaffold batches legal hex-grid paths through all ten digit masks, then bakes each path into a rod with rest lengths ell(s), natural bend angles kappa(s), and a two-sided hydropathy pattern. It is the current conformer generator and shared-soup testbed on the path toward many unfolded filaments where a keypress activates one selected fold. The temporal mode shows fixed-step overdamped angle dynamics driven by intrinsic curvature, thermal noise if enabled, soft excluded volume on all segments, and field-based hydropathy.

What this shows. The discrete stage chooses a topology: a single non-branching path through each digit domain. The selected temporal mode uses the baked material program directly: segment angles relax toward kappa(s), positions are reconstructed from fixed ell(s), both-side hydrophobic segments are blue, both-side hydrophilic amber, and mixed amphipathic segments green. Side samples apply field forces with a force couple: blue segments are buried, amber segments are exposed, and green segments rotate until their hydrophobic side faces inward and their hydrophilic side faces outward.
