Explore
  1. Observe. Follow one outline as its internal bundles extend and retract.
  2. Intervene. Tap the dish to leave an orange goal. Each cell rebuilds its dense front on the side facing it, then crawls there. Tap elsewhere and watch the fronts swing round.
  3. Inspect. Pause, then advance one step at a time. What would you measure to test the mechanism?

Ten interacting compartments. The goal sets each cell's polarity directly; this is an imposed cue, not a model of sensing. The network inside is 2D mechanics, not microscopy. They repel, move and deform, but do not divide.

Each cell has its own actin pool and membrane. The green tint is an illustrative density texture.

Keyboard: focus the dish, use arrow keys to steer and Space to pause. Computation pauses when this tab is hidden.
