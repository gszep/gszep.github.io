Simulated: each cell's membrane, its actin network and the forces between them; the two scents, which are released at membranes, spread, fade and cannot pass through cells; and what each cell reads of them around its own membrane. Nobody is told where anybody is.

Imposed: how a cell turns its front once it has read a side (its polarity is set directly; there is no signalling network inside); that the small cell stops running once about a third of it is wrapped; that resting cells have no network, keep round and give way when pushed; the stiff disc that stands for a nucleus; extra membrane for a cell that holds a prey; the joining of the two lips into a closed pocket; and the breakdown that begins inside it, where what is left is kept a round droplet. Also set by us: a running cell turns along what it leans on; the wall of the dish stops the hunter later than the others, so that a cup can close on a prey at the wall; where and when the next prey enters; and that the hunter slowly uses up what it ate. Everything is 2D mechanics with uncalibrated time, not microscopy.
